Hi all,

As the holiday season approaches, we wanted to let you know that our team will be operating at a reduced capacity from December 23rd through January 3rd, 2025.

If you have any expenses you would like to have paid out before the year ends, **please try to get them in** **this week**. We will process expenses after this week, but we can not guarantee they will follow the normal Tuesday / Thursday payout schedule.

We’ll still be here to support you, but response times may be longer than usual. For urgent matters, please don’t hesitate to contact us at hello@oscollective.org.
 

Thank you for your understanding, and we wish you a joyful holiday season!

\- Lauren, Ben, Sourav, and Grace
