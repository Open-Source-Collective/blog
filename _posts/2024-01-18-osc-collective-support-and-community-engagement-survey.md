3/1/24 update: Thank you to everyone who took our survey! I randomly drew 5 collectives using Google's Random Number Generator and I chose the following collectives:

1\. [https://opencollective.com/openaddresses](https://opencollective.com/openaddresses)
2\. [https://opencollective.com/roden](https://opencollective.com/roden)
3\. [https://opencollective.com/task](https://opencollective.com/task)
4\. [https://opencollective.com/jmonkeyengine](https://opencollective.com/jmonkeyengine)
5\. [https://opencollective.com/fosscontributors](https://opencollective.com/fosscontributors) 

At Open Source Collective, our mission is to sustain and support open source projects. We are committed to understanding the needs of our hosted collectives and how we can improve our support and services and as such, we'd like to invite you to fill out a survey. 

This brief survey is designed to collect your feedback on how we can best assist your projects, identify the resources that are most helpful to you, and understand your preferences for community engagement.

This survey will be open from January 18 - January 28.

To show our appreciation, OSC will donate $30 USD to five randomly selected collectives that participate in this survey. The OSC team will draw five collectives on Monday, January 29th, 2024. 

If you have any other questions, comments, concerns, or thoughts, please feel free to reach out to us at hello@oscollective.org.

Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts with us -- your input is greatly appreciated!
