Hello everyone,

We wanted to share a quick update on outgoing bank payments.

We are still actively working with Wise to resolve the issue affecting outbound payments. At this point, Wise has confirmed that the problem has progressed from the initial compliance review to a technical issue with the connection between Wise and our account. Their team is investigating, and we are in regular contact to restore service as quickly as possible.

### **Limited manual payout options**

While this issue is being resolved, we can manually process **some** payments more easily than others. Manual payouts are most feasible in the following cases:

-   **The recipient’s bank is in the United States**, or the recipient is **using Wise to receive funds** (for any expense, regardless of the $ amount)
-   **The expense currency is set to USD**, even if the recipient’s bank is outside the US and the amount is **over $5,000.**

The $5,000 expense limit is due to the $15-per-transfer fee for non-US banks. Wise's transfer fees are significantly lower, and if the expense is not time sensitive, you would save money by waiting until the Wise issue is resolved.

For expenses that require currency conversion, differences between Wise’s FX rates and our bank’s FX rates mean we cannot reliably guarantee that the amount received will exactly match the approved expense. In those situations, we believe it is better to wait until the Wise connection issue is resolved so we can process the expense accurately.

### **What we’re doing**

We are proactively reviewing expenses and will do our best to identify and process **approved expenses** that meet the criteria above. 

If you have an expense that:

-   Meets the criteria above, or
-   You would like us to prioritize paying this week

Please reach out to us at **hello@oscollective.org**, and we’ll take a look.

Expense submissions can continue as usual, and we will resume standard payouts as soon as Wise restores full service.

Thank you again for your patience—we know this is disruptive, and we appreciate your understanding while we work through this with Wise.
