Hello, OSC Community!

[GitHub Accelerator](https://accelerator.github.com/) is now accepting applications for its second cohort! This year's theme is 'Advancing AI' and their focus is on projects in several key areas:

\- AI Developer Tools and Infrastructure
\- Models and Frameworks (including architectures)
\- Hardware and Hardware Enablement
\- Data and Data Infrastructure
\-  Marketplaces and Tools for Democratizing Access to AI
\-  AI-Powered Developer Applications and Tools
\-  Cybersecurity

Selected participants will be awarded up to $40,000, along with support to enhance their projects.

We think this is a great opportunity for members of the Open Source Collective community so we've arranged an informational meeting with the GitHub Accelerator team, specifically for OSC-hosted projects.

**Meeting Details:**
**Date:** Tuesday, 27th of February
**Time:** 5:30 PM UTC (9:30 AM PT / 12:30 PM ET / 11 PM IST)
**RSVP:** [Register here to get video call link](https://opencollective.com/opensource/events/invitation-to-github-accelerator-informational-meeting-for-osc-community-5411f77e)

If you're interested in learning more, we encourage you to join us!
