### **PayPal Update**

We want to share an update about the use of PayPal for expense payments. While PayPal has been helpful for some payees, we've encountered ongoing challenges that have led us to reassess its role. As a result, we’re introducing a **limit of $600 USD per payout** for PayPal expenses. For larger amounts, you'll need to use a bank account in your name or set up a Wise account. This goes into effect the week of August 11, 2025.

We don’t anticipate significant disruption as more than half of payees already use Wise or a bank account, and our hosted member projects tend to prefer it due to lower fees and broader country coverage compared to PayPal. 

This change is part of our ongoing efforts to keep payment processing fair, transparent, and sustainable. We ask that projects inform their payees in advance if the payee typically uses PayPal, so they can plan accordingly.

If you have any questions or concerns, as always feel free to reach out at hello@oscollective.org.
