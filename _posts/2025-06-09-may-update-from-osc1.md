Hello, OSC community! We hope your June is off to a great start. We’re back with a monthly update, so without further ado, here are a few highlights from May!

### **📣 Announcements 📣**

**On the Sustain Podcast
**Ben Nickolls and Andrew Nesbitt were on the [Sustain](https://opencollective.com/sustainoss) podcast recently to talk about [Ecosyste.ms](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fecosyste.ms%2F), a project focused on improving how open source is understood and funded.

In the episode, they discuss how open source funders can use data to guide their funding decisions to help close the gap between funding the most critical open-source projects and those that tend to receive the most support, and more.

🎧 [Listen to the episode here
](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fpodcast.sustainoss.org%2F270)

### **✨ Community Highlights, Updates, and Announcements ✨**

**Mautic looking for a Senior Developer**

[Mautic](https://opencollective.com/mautic) is seeking a **freelance Senior Developer**, with the potential to transition into a long-term role. This is a remote position open to candidates within ±5 hours of the UTC timezone. This person should have experience in PHP development, particularly with Symfony and Doctrine.

💻 [Learn more about the Senior Developer opportunity with Mautic here.](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcommunity.mautic.org%2Fprocesses%2Fcontribute-to-mautic%2Ff%2F75%2Fposts%2F79)

**Welcome Newly Accepted Projects!**
