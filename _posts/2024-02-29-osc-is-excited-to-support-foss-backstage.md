If you are going or would like to attend their virtual program, please remember we will reimburse the cost of your ticket! More on how to do that [here](https://docs.oscollective.org/what-we-offer/grants-for-conference-attendance).

_FOSS Backstage Announcement below
_
Join us for the sixth edition of **FOSS Backstage** on **March 4th-5th**. The two-day conference will take place as a hybrid conference both **online and in Berlin** at our venue bUm. Talks will be streamed live, and virtual attendees can ask speakers questions.

With a focus on decentralized decision-making and open collaboration, FOSS Backstage will be a great place for developers, architects, sysadmins, community leaders, students, and anyone interested in building and managing communities within the open source space to get together and exchange ideas.

Find the **full program** here: [https://24.foss-backstage.de/schedule/](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2F24.foss-backstage.de%2Fschedule%2F)
We’re looking forward to meeting you!

And here’s how to join: Online and on-site tickets are still available on our [website](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2F24.foss-backstage.de%2Ftickets%2F) – we also have fully sponsored tickets through our [diversity and inclusion program](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2F24.foss-backstage.de%2Fdiversity-inclusion%2F).
