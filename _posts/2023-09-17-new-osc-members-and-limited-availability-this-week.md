Hi all!  You may have noticed two new names helping out around here. OSC is growing and we are excited to welcome two lovely folks to our team as Operations Coordinators. As you grow, so do we. Please say hello to Sourav Das and Grace Powers! :)

You might have seen Sourav on our Slack already offering support or guidance to others who use the OC platform BEFORE he even joined OSC.  Sourav has a background working with open source projects and nonprofits and we are excited to welcome him aboard! Sourav is based in Assam so if you are close or in that time zone, please say hi!

We are equally thrilled to welcome Grace Powers to the OSC team. Grace is located in sunny CA and has a background in Social Work and Open Source working with Mozilla in an HR and community management capacity.

With our new team, **we are taking the next week Sept. 18~22 to co-work and gather our thoughts for the next year**. We will be available on Slack and hello@oscollective.org but might not be available in the time zone you are used to. **_Expense payouts_** - We will still be processing expenses on Tuesday and Thursday but a majority will be processed on Thursday or toward the end of the week.

As always, if you need us don’t hesitate to ping us here on Slack or contact us over email at hello@oscollective.org 
Thanks! ~Lauren
