As part of our hosted project cleanup process, we are sharing this update here because it allows us to reach all hosted project admins in one final attempt. We are not tagging or naming individual projects in order to respect privacy and ensure each project has the opportunity to respond directly through the appropriate channels. 

> **Please note:** receiving this message does not necessarily mean your project was contacted as part of this process; this communication is being sent to all hosted project admins for transparency. 

### **Outreach Efforts**

Projects were included in this outreach if any of the following applied: 

-   No clear open source license was present on their linked repository
-   No visible activity on a linked repository for 1 year or more
-   The linked repository had been archived
-   No repository was linked to the project

We have used multiple communication channels to meet projects where they are, and projects that met our outreach criteria have already received at least three direct messages through Open Collective, email, or, in some instances, GitHub.  

### **What You Need to Do**

If you are an admin of a hosted member project, please check your inbox for messages from grace\[at\]oscollective\[dot\]org or hello\[at\]oscollective\[dot\]org with subject lines including:

-   Open Source Collective Check-In
-   Quick Check-In and Action Needed
-   Hi from Open Source Collective – Action Required
-   Fiscal Sponsorship Termination Notice

### **Deadline**

If you have received one of these messages, please respond by October 31, 2025 to maintain your fiscal hosting. Failure to do so may result in the termination of fiscal hosting. 

Thank you for your prompt attention.
