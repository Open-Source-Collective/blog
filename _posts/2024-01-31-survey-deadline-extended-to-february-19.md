Hello, OSC community! Just a quick note to announce that we're extending the deadline for our [hosted collective support survey](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2Fv8q4kS82HZo2fTV29). The new closing date for the survey is now **February 19th, 2024.**

As a reminder, here's what the [survey](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2Fv8q4kS82HZo2fTV29) is all about:

At OSC, our mission is to sustain and support open source projects. To better understand the needs of our hosted collectives and to enhance our support and services, we invite you to take [a short survey](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2Fv8q4kS82HZo2fTV29). This survey aims to gather feedback on how we can best assist your projects, identify the resources that are most helpful to you, and understand your preferences for community engagement.

**Updated survey period:** January 18 - February 19, 2024.

To show our appreciation, OSC will donate $30 USD to five randomly selected collectives who complete the survey. With the extension, the draw for these five collectives will now occur on Tuesday, February 20th, 2024.

Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts with us!

If you have any questions, comments, concerns, or additional thoughts, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at hello@oscollective.org.

OSC Collective Support and Community Engagement Survey: [](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2Fv8q4kS82HZo2fTV29)[https://forms.gle/v8q4kS82HZo2fTV29](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2Fv8q4kS82HZo2fTV29)
