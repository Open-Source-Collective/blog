Hey all,

We have some events coming up!

Our next **Community Call** will be this third Thursday, April 20th, at 12:00pm EST (9:00am PST, 5:00pm GMT).

**Sign Up:** You can [sign up to attend here.](https://opencollective.com/opensource/events/open-source-collective-community-call-april-22-3c8eb6cf)

**Agenda**: If you have anything you want to talk about with other collectives, add them to the agenda [here](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.google.com%2Fdocument%2Fd%2F1yCze-zkHFMpuG8sq5MeNNrxDi-WJyOtQwuf8JQPmBUk%2Fedit%23). That includes sharing about your work and successes you've had, and goals you're looking forward to working towards this year!

**Next events:** For those who want to know about future events, I've also added all of the Community Calls for the next few months with their times to [the OSC Events page](https://opencollective.com/opensource/events).

**Time zones:** If you would like to have calls at a different hour due to time zones, let me know. I'm happy to run another call at a different time.

**Calendar invite**: I have a calendar invite I am happy to extend to others. Hit me up at richard@opencollective.com and I'll invite you to it, if you want to keep that time blocked in your calendar!

As well, we have our first **New Collective Welcome Party for OSC Collectives** call next week, on Thursday, at 2:00pm EST (11:00am PST, 7:00pm GMT).

**Sign Up:** [You can sign up here.](https://opencollective.com/opensource/events/osc-new-collective-welcome-party-319e2049)

**Agenda**: We'll do a brief round of introductions for other collectives, and then go through and talk about how to set up your profile page, how to use the platform, what's next for your collective, and then set aside time for any questions you may have.

Looking forward to these!

Best,
Richard
