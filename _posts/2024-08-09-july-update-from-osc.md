### 📣 Announcements

**OSC can now accept Polar payments!**

Learn more here: [https://docs.oscollective.org/campaigns-programs-and-partnerships/polas.sh](https://docs.oscollective.org/campaigns-programs-and-partnerships/polas.sh)

**Collective Review**

We finished our first round of the collective review process. In the first round, we identified hosted projects with 0 balance and no incoming funds or outgoing expense activity in over a year. After emailing project admins to confirm in writing if they needed the project to remain active, we unhosted it after one month of waiting for a response. In total, we unhosted over 1,300 projects.

We are moving on to the second phase of our cleanup to identify projects that might not be in compliance with our terms of fiscal hosting. Here are some of the things we will be looking at during the review:

-   Missing URLs on OC profile page to code repositories or community events
-   Lack of activity for the past 2 years (events or in repo)
-   No license on the repositories

As before, we will email any collective identifying areas for clarification. We’re happy to discuss any exceptions; however, we need to ensure the remaining projects are in compliance. We plan to conduct this review annually and hope it helps us learn more about your projects and identify ways we can better support you.

**United Nations OSPO for Good Summit**

Earlier in the month, we attended the OSPO for Good summit in NY. There were a lot of great talks and the full video archive is available here - [https://joinup.ec.europa.eu/collection/open-source-observatory-osor/news/ospos-good-2024-highlights](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fjoinup.ec.europa.eu%2Fcollection%2Fopen-source-observatory-osor%2Fnews%2Fospos-good-2024-highlights). One of the points that Lauren, our executive director, is still thinking about is Aviva Black’s closing in the Open Source and Governments track:

> “It’s not just open source code, but it needs to be open roadmap, open process, and open community.”
