Open Source Collective's monthly community call is this **Thursday, November 16 @ 4:00 UTC (9:30pm IT, 4:00pm BT, 12:00pm ET, and 9:00am PT)**. This month, we want to hear from you: what is going well, what could be better, what you're working on, how your experience with us as your fiscal host has been. 

 **Register:** RSVP and receive the video link [here](https://opencollective.com/opensource/events/open-source-collective-community-call-november-23-143bc6df). 

**Calendar invite:** do you want to block time on your calendar? Email me at grace@opencollective.com and I can invite you to the calendar invite.

**Any questions or concerns?** Email me at [grace@opencollective.com](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=mailto%3Agrace%40opencollective.com).

Looking forward to sharing virtual space with you soon!
