Thank you to everyone who joined the first Open Source Collective Community Call last night. For those of you who couldn't attend a [recording is available for the next 30 days](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fus06web.zoom.us%2Frec%2Fshare%2FryuAv9AMnfYBTV_sstIEjLY82tAF11u622e6ElH4X3wyPzcY0HTPUZH2pDkyAewT.pGzYNEQsbJggm0gM%3FstartTime%3D1631812788000).

In this conversation we covered:

-   why we (Open Source Collective) want to build a strong, more community-centred fiscal host, and what we hope to achieve with these regular conversations, 
-   approaches to paying contributors,
-   defining maintenance and approaches to valuing non-code contributions,
-   building inclusive and diverse communities and,
-   increasing a contributor base and improving developer experience by pairing with new contributors.

Matt Wynne from [Cucumber](https://opencollective.com/cucumber) shared that he is looking for guidance on how to manage contributor payments. Ruth Cheesley from [Mautic](https://opencollective.com/mautic) shared Mautic’s [policy on paying contributors](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontribute.mautic.org%2Fpolicies%2Fpaying-contributors) and details of [Mautic's partner program](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontribute.mautic.org%2Fpolicies%2Fmautic-community-partners-program), including how they level the entry requirements based on geography with [the Big Mac Adjusted method](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.google.com%2Fspreadsheets%2Fd%2F1VctW_ZSySbnKpgfR4981qmUJUkglqP-ddwU8pclQMoA%2Fedit%3Fusp%3Dsharing%2521).

Ruth also shared some information about [https://www.savannahhq.com/](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.savannahhq.com%2F) which Mautic uses to better understand how members of the community are contributing outside of committing code.

I shared some information about these calls, about what I would like us to use these spaces for and hinted at a couple of issues I would like to address within these conversations. Some of which can be found [in my notes](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.google.com%2Fdocument%2Fd%2F1uDxOfmuXR0EFaUGA0llP5W9j8TDo65oRzMSKkSW-GAY%2Fedit%23heading%3Dh.cy9u8rkplbrjI). I also picked up on [an interesting conversation that I had with Nickolas Zakas about their experience of paying contributors on ESLint](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Feslint.org%2Fblog%2F2020%2F10%2Fyear-paying-contributors-review). it makes for an interesting read and I love that their approach includes _many_ types of contribution within the process. I am going to encourage Nicholas to be on the Sustain podcast [https://podcast.sustainoss.org/](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fpodcast.sustainoss.org%2F) (which is an excellent resource) to talk about their experience.

For me, this really was a great start to a process that I’d love to continue with maintainers of projects hosted by Open Source Collective.
