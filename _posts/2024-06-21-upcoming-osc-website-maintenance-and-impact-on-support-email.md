We would like to update you on a forthcoming maintenance period for our [website](https://oscollective.org/). This maintenance is scheduled to take place from 3 AM EDT to 5 AM EDT on June 25, 2024. During this window, our support email ([hello@oscollective.org](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=mailto%3Ahello%40oscollective.org)) may experience temporary downtime.

If you try to contact us via our support email at [hello@oscollective.org](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=mailto%3Ahello%40oscollective.org) during this maintenance window and do not receive a response from us, we kindly ask that you write to us again once the maintenance is completed. We understand that this interruption can cause some inconvenience and we truly appreciate your understanding and patience.
