_Today we're_ [_launching a fund_](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine#about) _to centralise support from within Open Source Collective for a number of humanitarian and journalistic integrity initiatives concerning the war in Ukraine._

Open Source Collective exists to promote a sustainable and healthy ecosystem to sustain open source technology for the future. We believe that the current conflict in Ukraine threatens our ecosystem and we encourage Collectives hosted by Open Source Collective to contribute to support humanitarian and journalistic integrity initiatives.

This fund simplifies the process of donating to these initiatives from your Collective — simply [select your Collective in the 'Contribute As' step](https://docs.opencollective.com/help/financial-contributors/financial-contributors#can-my-collective-give-to-another-collective), you will need to be an administrator of a collective to do this. Note that donations to the fund from individuals will be rejected.

\- **To donate to all of the initiatives listed** please [contribute](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine/donate) using your Collective as the contributor, the fund will contribute equally to each initiative.

\- **To contribute to a specific initiative** click the individual links listed below. 

-   [The Ukrainian Red Cross](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine/projects/the-ukrainian-red-cross/donate)
-   [The International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine/projects/red-cross-red-crescent/donate)
-   [The Ukrainian Hospitaliers](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine/projects/the-ukrainian-hospitaliers/donate) (a parliamentary field medical unit deployed alongside troops)
-   [Ranzom for Ukraine](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine/projects/ranzom-for-ukraine/donate) (emergency medical response)
-   [The Voices of Children Campaign](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine/projects/voices-of-children)
-   [The Kyiv Independent](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine/projects/the-kyiv-independent)
-   [The 24.02 Journalism emergency Fund](https://opencollective.com/support-ukraine/projects/2402-journalism-emergency-fund)

We will begin donating from this fund on a weekly, ongoing basis from _this Friday_.

Our thanks go out to [the Jest community](https://opencollective.com/jest), who have started the fund with a $10,000 donation alongside a $10,000 donation from our own organisation.
