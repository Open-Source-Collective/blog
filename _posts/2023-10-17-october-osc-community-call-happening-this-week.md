Open Source Collective's monthly community call is this **Thursday, October 19 @ 4:00 UTC (9:30pm IT, 5:00pm BT, 12:00pm ET, and 9:00am PT)**. This month, Serkan Holat will be joining and discussing his open-source-focused funding and policy work. This will be the second to last meeting of 2023!

 **Register:** RSVP and receive the video link [here](https://opencollective.com/opensource/events/open-source-collective-community-call-october-23-d91ab869). 

**Calendar invite:** do you want to block time on your calendar? Email me at grace@opencollective.com and I can invite you to the calendar invite.

**Agenda:** Something you want to talk about? Any recent wins or blockers? Add them by commenting on the WiP agenda [here](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.google.com%2Fdocument%2Fd%2F1ZrO_w8bemLUlQVHshxRXDD_U0FmhNaDM1NCVPld_Ono%2Fedit%3Fusp%3Dsharing).

**Any questions or concerns?** Email me at [grace@opencollective.com](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=mailto%3Agrace%40opencollective.com).

Looking forward to sharing virtual space with you soon!
