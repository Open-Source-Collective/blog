The Open Source Collective team is gathering in Berlin, and while we’re here, we’re hosting a meetup for our community. If you’re in or around Berlin on **Monday, 23 September**, we’d love for you to join us at a local biergarten for drinks, snacks, and great conversation. It’ll be a laid-back evening and an opportunity to connect with OSC and others in our community. 

Just fill out [this form](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2FSD2hBPwyZAYzXkMt9), and we’ll send you the details.

We hope to see you there!
