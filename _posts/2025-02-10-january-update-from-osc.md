Hello, OSC community! Happy FOSS February!

Our team is calling 2025 **the year of conversations.** We're getting out into the community more -- both IRL and online -- and ramping up how we engage with our hosted Collectives. Below, you'll find updates on where we've been, where we're headed, and where we can connect. We're excited for more conversations in the months ahead!
 
**📣 Announcements & Events 📣**

 
**FOSDEM recap: “Open source funding: you’re doing it wrong”**
FOSDEM happened! As part of the event, OSC’s Ben and [Ecosyste.ms](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=http%3A%2F%2FEcosyste.ms)’ Andrew delivered a talk titled _"Open source funding: you’re doing it wrong."_ Their presentation argues that observable usage best defines which projects are 'critical' and that we need better, open data about the direct and indirect support for these projects in order to coordinate our efforts to sustain them.

Video, slides (and cute dogs) can be found [here](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.fosdem.org%2F2025%2Fevents%2Fattachments%2Ffosdem-2025-5576-open-source-funding-you-re-doing-it-wrong%2Fslides%2F237729%2FOpen_Sour_m6JM6N9.pdf).

**Sustain Virtual Forum on March 13th**
It's happening again!

If you have interest, passion, or need around the diverse range of topics collectively described as “sustainability of free and open source software,” we invite you to join us for the SustainOSS Virtual Forum on Thursday, 13 March 2025.

At the SustainOSS Virtual Forum, we will survey the challenges, evolution, and surprises recurrently navigated but rarely documented or collectively validated in running open source projects, program offices, or policy networks populated by researchers, developers, and policymakers. Above all, we invite you to share your experiences, ideas, and concerns around sustainability across a range of scales, engage in respectful discussion, and envision goals and partnerships we can collectively embrace to sustain our open source ecosystems.

The interactive agenda will be co-designed in collaboration with confirmed participants in the time leading up to the event.

We encourage you to join us as we explore the who, the what, and the how of realizing a sustainable present and future for open source. And we thank you for helping us spread the word!

Register in the link below and please share with others: [https://opencollective.com/sustainoss/events/sustainoss-virtual-forum-348aa185](https://opencollective.com/sustainoss/events/sustainoss-virtual-forum-348aa185)

**Apereo's** **February MicroConference: "Unlocking Open Source Grant Funding Opportunities” on February 12th**
Apereo's \[1\] February MicroConference will be "Unlocking Open Source Grant Funding Opportunities”. The speaker, Josh Baron \[2\], currently serves as Apereo's Development Officer and has a wealth of business development and fundraising experience including with the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation as a Senior Program Officer.
 
Session highlights:
 

-   Strategies for Grant Funding Success: Understand critical differences between federal/national funding opportunities, private foundation grants, and tailored strategies for each.
-   2025 Funding Trends and Opportunities: Discover the current priorities of grant-makers and explore upcoming funding opportunities tailored to open source initiatives.
-   Apereo’s “Funding Prioritization Framework:" Gain access to this newly developed tool to help organizations effectively assess and prioritize funding opportunities.
-   Interactive Q&A Session: The webinar will conclude with a 15-minute live Q&A, offering attendees the chance to address their pressing questions by an expert in the field.

Date: Wednesday, February 12, 2025 Time: 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM Eastern US Time Location: Virtual (Big Blue Button link provided upon registration)

**Sequelize Needs Maintainers**

Sequelize is one of the most widely used ORMs for Node.js, helping developers interact with databases efficiently. They are preparing for a major new release but need help getting it across the finish line. In addition to working on a high-impact open-source project, they are offering a stipend for your contributions. Read more about it in their announcement [here](https://opencollective.com/sequelize/updates/join-the-sequelize-team-help-shape-the-future-of-node-js-orm).
