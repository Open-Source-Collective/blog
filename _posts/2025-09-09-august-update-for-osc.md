**ICYMI: Public Service Announcement Re. Salt Typhoon**

Yesterday, [OSC published a post about Salt Typhoon.](https://opencollective.com/opensource/updates/public-service-announcement-re-salt-typhoon) TLDR: The US and its allies have declared a national defense crisis. Critical open source dependencies are an attack vector for state actors. We recommend that you contact your mobile service provider to lock down your account and use biometric-protected passkeys instead of relying solely on SMS as a two-factor authentication solution. 

**What to do:**

1.  **Secure your mobile account:** Contact your provider and set up a passphrase to authorize any changes to your account
2.  **Use strong authentication:** Wherever possible, protect accounts with biometric-enabled passkeys, especially accounts with access to commit and distribution
3.  **Avoid SMS for 2FA:** Don’t rely solely on text messages for two-factor authentication

For more details, see [our post](https://opencollective.com/opensource/updates/public-service-announcement-re-salt-typhoon). But above all, take action now to secure your accounts.

**Hosted Project Review and Outreach**

Behind the scenes, we’ve been reviewing hosted member project repositories to better understand which projects may no longer be active or may no longer meet our hosting policies. With more than 2,500 projects under fiscal hosting, it’s a big effort that takes time and care. We're grateful for your understanding if you’ve been contacted as part of this process. Once the review wraps up, we’ll share more about how we conducted this review and what we learned.

**PayPal and Expenses**

As an update from [last month](https://opencollective.com/opensource/updates/july-update-for-osc): in order to continue offering PayPal for expenses, we’ve put a new practice in place around payee verification. For now, payments can still be made through PayPal, but the process may now involve a few extra steps for both OSC and the payee. If you or one of your payees receives expenses via PayPal and will need to go through one of these extra steps, the OSC team will let you know and walk you through these additional steps.

If you’re an admin of a hosted project, please let your payees know that while PayPal remains an option for most individuals, using a bank account is often faster and can save the project money on third-party fees.

We’re continuing to monitor this and exploring how we can best offer flexible payment options while working within the realities of nonprofit requirements and global compliance. This process is a work in progress and may change over time.
