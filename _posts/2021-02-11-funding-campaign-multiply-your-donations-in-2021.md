In March, we’re partnering with GitCoin to launch FUNDOSS, a pilot matching campaign based on a democratic funding model. This funding model has already raised over [$11M for over 7,000 projects](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fgitcoin.co%2Fresults) in the web3 space. Let’s see what we can do in ours.

Want in? [SIGN UP](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2FU4xUwhTBgXxhGEte6) now to be considered for the pilot.

## **What’s in it for you?**

-   **Grow your budget**: Matching is based on the number of donations not the number of the bill. The more donations the greater the multiplier effect. Ten people giving $1 can go much further than 1 person giving $10.Activate your networks to activate a match. ([VIDEO](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DHJljTtLnymE%26feature%3Demb_title) explaining it all!)

-   **Support your dependencies.** Donations are about ecosystem sustainability not just your own. With democratic funding, you can provide crowdfunding for your entire tree. So everyone wins – including you. 

-   **Refer a friend**: [Share](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2FU4xUwhTBgXxhGEte6) some democratic funding love to a project not yet part of OSC. For new collectives selected to be part of FUNDOSS – you get a free $10 Gift Card. Use it during the match to turn $10 into more. 

FUNDOSS brings crowdfunded agency to the sustainability of open source. You can learn more about the math behind our democratic matching model [HERE](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwtfisqf.com%2F%3Fgrant%3D%26grant%3D%26grant%3D%26grant%3D%26match%3D1000)!
