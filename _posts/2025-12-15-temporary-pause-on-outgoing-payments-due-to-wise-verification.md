Hello everyone,

We want to let you know that **outgoing bank payments are temporarily on hold** while our payments provider, Wise, completes a routine AML/KYC re-verification of Open Source Collective. 

From time to time, Wise requires organizations to re-verify identity and governance information as part of their regulatory obligations. As a nonprofit fiscal host, this process applies at the organizational level and involves verification of OSC’s leadership. While this review is underway, Wise has placed a temporary pause on outbound payments. We hope to complete this process by the end of the week at the latest and we will immediately process all pending payments once this is complete.

**What this means for collectives:**

-   Expense submissions can continue as normal.
-   OSC will process approved expenses, add them to the payment queue, and pay them once the review is complete.
-   No action is required from collectives at this time.

We are actively working with Wise to complete the verification as quickly as possible and will notify you as soon as outbound payments resume. We understand this may cause inconvenience, and appreciate your patience while this standard compliance process is completed.

If you have urgent concerns or questions, please reach out to us at **hello@oscollective.org**.

Thank you for your understanding and for being part of the OSC community.

Warmly,

Open Source Collective - Lauren, Ben, Sourav, Grace & Caitlin
