###
**GitHub Sponsors Payout Update**

If you receive GitHub Sponsorsors funding through Open Source Collective, you may have noticed a smll additional amount reflected in your project's balance. This deposit was due to an issue GitHub Sponsors experienced on their end with payouts to OSC in July and September 2025, which resulted in a small number of Collectives receiving slightly less than their expected payout amounts for those months. OSC identified the issue and raised it with the Github Sponsors team, who resolved the issue. The missing funds have since been allocated.
 
We appreciate the GitHub Sponsors team for resolving the issue. If you have any questions, please reach out.

### **Invoices and Supplier Portals** 

As your Collective plans their 2026 funding strategy, please note that OSC can issue formal invoices and submit invoices through a variety of supplier and procurement portals. Being able to meet these requirements can help unlock funding from organizations that are only able to pay via invoice or require vendors to go through formal procurement systems. If you’re speaking with a potential funder who has requested either of these requirements, or think it might open up an opportunity for your project, please reach out.

### **FOSDEM Talk**

At FOSDEM, our very own Benjam joined the wonderful Shannon from Open Collective to share the story of how the Open Collective platform transitioned from a for-profit company to a nonprofit, and where we, OSC, fit into that equation. 

People often assume OSC and Open Collective are one and the same, but that’s not quite the case. The talk does a great job unpacking the different entities and how they relate to one another. 

If you’ve ever wondered who does what, the governance models at play, how we collaborate while remaining distinct orgs, and what changed with the new model, this session should help connect the dots.

You can watch here: [https://mirror.cyberbits.eu/fosdem/2026/ud2218a/UQUCFW-the\_buyout\_how\_the\_open\_source\_community\_liberated\_its\_own\_platform.av1.webm](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmirror.cyberbits.eu%2Ffosdem%2F2026%2Fud2218a%2FUQUCFW-the_buyout_how_the_open_source_community_liberated_its_own_platform.av1.webm)
