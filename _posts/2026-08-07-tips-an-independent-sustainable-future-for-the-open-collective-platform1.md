_You are receiving this message because you are an administrator of at least one project hosted by Open Source Collective on the Open Collective platform. We are letting you know that you may see a request for a 'platform tip' on a small proportion of donations to your Collective(s) over the coming weeks as we explore an independent way to sustain the platform on which we all depend. Read on for more...
_
**Background**
Open Source Collective (OSC) is the non-profit organisation that manages the financial, operational, and legal requirements on behalf of over 2,500 open source communities, including yours. In 2024, we led the acquisition of the Open Collective platform from Open Collective Inc. The Open Collective platform is now owned and operated by Open Finance Consortium (OFiCo): a non-profit consortium of its biggest users, all of whom are themselves non-profits.

Since the acquisition, OSC has provided significant financial support to OFiCo as it finds its feet and re-launches its core product. That support has come with a meaningful cost to OSC, limiting the services we can offer and our capacity to engage in initiatives that matter to our member projects. Now, as OSC reduces its financial commitment, OFiCo is taking steps to establish itself as a financially independent, sustainable non-profit by turning to the one funding source that can reliably make up the difference: optional tips.

Over the decade, optional tips have proven to be the most cost-effective, reliable, and sustainable way to cover the cost of maintaining the Open Collective platform. Open Source Collective is the only host organisation not to include a request for an optional tip in the contribution flow. We are also the largest host organisation on the platform, receiving US $6m in crowdfunding contributions per year on behalf of our member projects. Requesting an optional tip on those donations lowers the cost of using the platform for communities like yours and hosts like ours.

**What we’re doing**
Over the past six weeks, we have tested tips with six of our largest open source communities. Our data shows that contributors tip with no statistically significant reduction in contributions to the receiving Collective. These findings align with data we see across the platform, including within open source communities hosted by other organisations. Most importantly, projections show that donors to OSC-hosted projects would collectively cover over half of the annual cost to develop and maintain the platform.

**Next steps**
The next phase of our test will corroborate these results at scale, demonstrating to our member Collectives that requesting an optional tip is a near-zero-risk way of supporting the platform on which we all depend. Over the next few weeks, you may see a request for an optional tip on contributions to your Collective while we collect more data from a small proportion of donations across all our Collectives.

**Our promise to member projects**
We are doing all we can to ensure that our member Collectives will not be adversely impacted by this change while still providing for an independent, sustainable future for the platform on which we all depend. If we see a demonstrable negative impact on donations to our projects, we will pursue alternative arrangements.

With this approach, the money OSC saves on platform costs will be reinvested in our mission: developing our services, investing significantly in our public policy, tools, support, and other initiatives intended to keep our collectives' overhead low. That's the outcome we're aiming for, and it's the primary reason we're moving to tips.

**Feedback**
If a tip in your donation flow is a genuine dealbreaker for you, one that would make you consider leaving Open Source Collective, please tell us. That's not an outcome we want. We'd rather talk it through with you than lose you so please, email notips@opensourcecollective.org to discuss this with the team.
