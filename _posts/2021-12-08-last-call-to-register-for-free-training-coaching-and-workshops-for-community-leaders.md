_We are offering a limited number of places to leaders of projects hosted by Open Source Collective on a series of coaching, training, and workshops designed to support you and your projects. We will close registrations at 6pm UTC on Friday 10th December. To register your interest please complete_ [_this registration form_](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.google.com%2Fforms%2Fd%2Fe%2F1FAIpQLSfvGzE4XsSjYPGxP8Aq66WZpeR7w2Y5XSXsN1L2zu115-1x7w%2Fviewform)_.

_On November 25th [I announced](https://opencollective.com/opensource/updates/training-coaching-and-workshops-for-community-leaders) that we would offer a limited number of places to leaders of projects hosted by Open Source Collective on a series of coaching, training, and workshop sessions. These sessions are designed to work through common problems and to help answer questions like 'how do we bring new contributors?', 'what do we spend our money on?', and 'who gets to make decisions?'. We've received a lot of responses but we thought it would be best to give those of you who might have missed our initial announcement a second chance.

We'll be closing registration this Friday the 10th December at 6pm UTC. This will enable us to get started: selecting participants, building the program, and _potentially_ make use of the time available to participants over the holiday period.

If you're interested in taking part in single sessions or throughout the 8 session program please complete [this registration form](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.google.com%2Fforms%2Fd%2Fe%2F1FAIpQLSfvGzE4XsSjYPGxP8Aq66WZpeR7w2Y5XSXsN1L2zu115-1x7w%2Fviewform) before 6pm UTC this Friday 10th December.
