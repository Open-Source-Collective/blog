Hello, OSC community! We hope your July is off to a wonderful start. Below you will find OSC's monthly update for June. Keep reading for announcements, community updates, and a funding opportunity for collectives in Germany!

### 📣 **Announcements**

**Berlin (or Vienna) Meetup!**

Are you in or around Berlin or attending the Open Source Summit in Vienna? OSC is considering hosting a happy hour or meetup and we’re gauging interest. If you’re in or around those cities, let us know by the end of July if you’re interested by filling out the [Berlin/Vienna OSC Meetup Interest Survey](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fforms.gle%2FS7dnmGUiW7FnozW56)

**Discord**

Open Collective has moved from Slack to Discord! Join Open Source Collective and the entire OC community at [http://discord.opencollective.com](http://discord.opencollective.com)

**FOSSY 2024 in Portland, Oregon**

Software Freedom Conservancy’s [FOSSY 2024](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2F2024.fossy.us%2F) is happening August 1-4th 2024 in Portland, Oregon USA. Want to attend but can’t afford a ticket? Don’t forget that OSC offers [grants for conference attendance](https://docs.oscollective.org/what-we-offer/grants-for-conference-attendance)!

**Open Source Funding Survey**

GitHub, The Linux Foundation, and Harvard University researchers are studying how organizations support open source software. If you work in an OSPO, as a  Head of Engineering or Product, a C-Level Executive, or are knowledgeable about your organization's open source involvement, check out their survey: [2024 Open Source Software Funding Survey](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fhbs.qualtrics.com%2Fjfe%2Fform%2FSV_eKVt69Ek3H2yYx8%3FSource%3DGeneric_Email)

**ICYMI: OSC is** **consistently paying maintainers $1m a month
**We wrote about this incredible milestone [here](https://opencollective.com/dashboard/opensource/updates/zzaxon79-3jy8gpl3-r8jpbrkd-emwl5v04)!

Mautic's Virtual Conference!**

Mautic’s [fully virtual global conference](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmauticon.mautic.org%2Fen%2Fglobal%2Fmcg-24%2F) is happening 10-11 July 2024! If you want to learn what you can do with open source marketing stacks, check it out! Tickets range from $10-$60 and 50% of the ticket cost gets allocated to Mautic’s dependencies. Want to attend but your collective doesn’t have enough funds? Don’t forget that OSC offers [grants for conference attendance!](https://docs.oscollective.org/what-we-offer/grants-for-conference-attendance)

**DocDetective at Open Source Summit and Their New Shop!**

Check out Manny from DocDetective’s talk at the Open Source Summit, [Docs as Tests: No More Broken Docs](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3D6qlRhPRD1DM%26ab_channel%3DTheLinuxFoundation). DocDetective also recently opened up a swag shop and you can get your own No More Broken Docs shirt here [here](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fshop.doc-detective.com%2F)!

**Play Framework at WeAreDevelopers World Congress!**

Are you attending the [WeAreDevelopers World Congress](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wearedevelopers.com%2Fworld-congress%2Ftickets) from July 17-19 in Berlin? If so, stop by and say hello to Play Framework! They’ll be at the Github Booth as part of the [GitHub Open Source Spotlight](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wearedevelopers.com%2Fworld-congress%2Fgithub-open-source-spotlight)!
