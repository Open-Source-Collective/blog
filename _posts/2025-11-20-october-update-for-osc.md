### **📣 Announcements 📣
**
**Free Security Keys**

Thanks to a generous donation from Google — and following our [recent security recommendations](https://opencollective.com/opensource/updates/public-service-announcement-re-salt-typhoon) — we’re able to offer free Titan Security Keys ([https://cloud.google.com/security/products/titan-security-key](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcloud.google.com%2Fsecurity%2Fproducts%2Ftitan-security-key)) to our hosted collectives so you can share them with your community.

Because we’ll be mailing these, shipping costs vary widely. We are limiting sending **10 or more** keys to a single address. If shipping to your location turns out to be prohibitively expensive, we may not be able to fulfill the request.

If your collective would like keys, please email **hello@oscollective.org** with:

-   your mailing address
-   how many keys you’d like

### **Funding Opportunity: Neon Sponsorship for Open Source Projects**

Neon is currently sponsoring open source projects through their Neon Open Source Program, offering:

-   $5,000 in platform credits yearly
-   Payments through GitHub Sponsors
-   Promotional help 

Their criteria is: 

1.  Open Source - Your work must be open source and publicly available under a recognized open-source license. Developers should be able to self-host the software.
2.  Runs on Postgres - Open-source software that uses Postgres is an ideal fit for this program. This could be anything from using Postgres as a component in a larger stack to Postgres extensions.
3.  Using Neon - Provide documentation, CLI tools, and code examples that teach your users how to build with your software and Neon.

More information can be found here: [https://neon.com/blog/neon-open-source-program](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fneon.com%2Fblog%2Fneon-open-source-program)

### **Who’s Hiring? An amazing opportunity to support Open Source** 

The Sovereign Tech Agency is looking for a new Head of Sovereign Tech Fund, the internationally recognised and referenced programme investing in critical software projects. Since its launch in 2022 STF has supported hundreds of critical open source projects, distributing tens of millions in financial support, some of which we accept for use by our member projects. 

If you’re fluent in English and German, and interested to find out more, you can check out the role on [STA’s website](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sovereign.tech%2Fjobs%2Fhead-of-sovereign-tech-fund).
