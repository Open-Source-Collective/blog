Hey everyone,

A bit late this month, but I wanted to let you all know that our monthly community call is coming up on Thursday! This is where we hold a space for you to learn from other OSC collective admins and collaborators, to talk to OSC staff, and to hear about new updates on the platform.

[Join us for the next event, this Thursday](https://opencollective.com/opensource/events/open-source-collective-community-call-july-22-07069399)! It'll be at 1700 UTC: 6:00pm BST, 5:00pm CET, 1:00pm ET, and 10:00am PT. Let us know if you can't make it, and we'll be happy to change the times up in the future.

Last month, we had a load of newcomers, and so talked largely about how to use the platform, how we could best learn from collectives in other regions, and about updates to our global payment processors (mostly: Payoneer makes this easy, now!). The video from the last call is available for a short time, [here](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fyoutu.be%2FWHbXjpl9ZNQ).

As always: if you have news you want to share, and especially if your Collective has invoiced or worked with another Open Collective this month, let us know! We want to start building a lattice of interconnected collectives working together - learning how this network is growing would be amazing, and allows us to share your work, too. Get in touch.

I'm looking forward to sharing this space this month, and hope you can make it.

Best,
Richard
