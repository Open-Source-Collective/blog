**_\*Thank you for your interest and support. The application window for this position is now closed\*_**

Open Source Collective is looking for a thoughtful, strategic problem-solver who brings empathy to systems thinking and process improvement.

As our **Senior Operations Manager**, you'll lead across three core operational pillars: **Finances, Support, and Operational Efficiency**. This is a proactive, senior individual contributor role ideal for someone who thrives in a remote, asynchronous work environment and enjoys streamlining systems, supporting communities, and financial sustainability. You don’t need to be an expert in both open source or nonprofits, but it helps if you have experience in either and are excited to support a global network of projects doing meaningful work.

The ideal candidate is familiar with our work, [our mission](https://docs.oscollective.org/mission-values-and-strategy#mission), and can demonstrate [our values](https://docs.oscollective.org/about/what-is-osc#how-we-think) in their own work.  

This role is advertised as a full-time, remote position based within 5 hours of the EST timezone. Compensation will be between $95,000 – $120,000 USD annually, plus benefits, contingent on experience.

If this sounds like you, please follow the link below to learn more about the role and apply. Applications will only be accepted through the form below. We are accepting applications until (and not including) 28th April.

Feel free to share this message with anyone you believe would be a good fit, but please do not contact us if you are a recruiter.
