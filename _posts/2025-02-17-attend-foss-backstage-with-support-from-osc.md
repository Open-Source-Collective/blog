ICYM our [Bluesky](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fbsky.app%2Fprofile%2Foscollective.org), [LinkedIn](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Fcompany%2Fopensourcecollective), and [Mastodon](https://opencollective.com/redirect?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmastodon.social%2F%40OpenSourceCollective) posts last week...

Are you a maintainer or another representative of an OSC-hosted Collective? Want to attend **FOSS Backstage on 10-11 of March**, but your project doesn’t have the budget for a ticket?

We can (probably) help!

FOSS Backstage is all about community, management, and compliance in open source, and we’re proud to sponsor it again this year. If you’ll be in or around Berlin and want to attend in person, or if you prefer to join virtually from anywhere, OSC is offering ticket support for projects who need it.

Interested? Let us know by the **27** **February.
